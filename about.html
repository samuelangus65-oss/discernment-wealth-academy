About Discernment Wealth Academy

Financial ideas should be explained clearly, without judgement, unnecessary jargon or pressure to buy something. Discernment Wealth Academy helps you understand your money and make informed decisions, whatever your starting point.

Why I built it

I am Samuel Angus, founder of Discernment Wealth Academy, a financial educator and qualified coach and mentor.

I spent more than twenty years working in the National Health Service across carer support, equality and diversity, service management and family-focused practice. My training in systemic family therapy also shaped how I understand people, relationships and change.

While supporting carers, I helped people successfully apply for respite grants. The support was available, but finding it and completing the application could be difficult. Clear explanations and practical help made a difference.

My committee roles brought another perspective. At the Leeds Christian Community Trust and another third sector organisation, I helped consider decisions about community funding, staffing, assets and surplus funds. These responsibilities taught me to weigh competing needs, ask careful questions and consider the future.

These experiences helped shape the Academy: a place where people can learn about money, ask questions and take practical steps without being made to feel they should already know the answers.

Why Ecclesiastes 11:2

“Give a portion to seven, and also to eight; for thou knowest not what evil shall be upon the earth.”

Ecclesiastes 11:2, King James Version

My Christian faith is part of why I built the Academy. I draw from this verse a principle of preparation: we cannot know everything the future will bring, so we should think carefully about how we use and share what we have.

In financial education, that means exploring emergency savings, understanding debt and avoiding unnecessary reliance on one investment or source of income.

The verse is a guiding principle, rather than a formula for dividing your money into a fixed number of accounts or investments. Your circumstances matter.

You are welcome here whether or not you share my faith.

Discernment, not dependence

Discernment means looking carefully, asking useful questions and weighing your options before making a decision.

The Academy’s purpose is to help you develop that understanding for yourself. It provides general financial education, rather than personalised financial advice or promises about what your money will earn.

Each Foundations lesson follows a clear sequence:

  • Learn: understand the idea.
  • See: work through an example.
  • Reflect: consider what it means for you.
  • Act: take a manageable step.
  • Record: keep something useful to return to.

You can work at your own pace and revisit the lessons as your circumstances change.

A free starting point

Optional workbooks, live support and advanced programmes may carry a charge. You can use the free lessons without buying them.

Whether you are starting from scratch or reviewing habits you have held for years, the aim is the same: clearer understanding and more informed decisions.
