Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

This policy explains what personal data Discernment Wealth Academy collects, why, and what your rights are. It is written in plain English, since a privacy policy nobody can actually understand is not much of a policy.

Who we are

Discernment Wealth Academy is operated by Samuel Angus, a sole trader based in Leeds, United Kingdom. For anything relating to privacy or your personal data, contact contact@discernmentwealthacademy.co.uk.

Data protection registration: Discernment Wealth Academy is in the process of registering with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, as required for businesses that collect personal data. The registration reference will be added here once confirmed.

What we collect

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technology. The only personal data collected is your email address, and only if you choose to enter it after completing the "Your Relationship With Money" reflection tool, in order to receive your result by email. If you do not enter your email address, nothing about you is collected or stored anywhere.

Why we collect it

Your email address is used to send you the personalised result you asked for, along with occasional, relevant updates about Discernment Wealth Academy's free resources and courses. The lawful basis for this is your consent, given by voluntarily entering your email address and submitting the form. You can withdraw that consent and unsubscribe at any time.

How it is stored

This site has no database of its own. Email addresses submitted through the reflection tool are processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. Formspree's own privacy policy governs how they store and protect that data, available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

How long we keep it

Your email address is kept until you unsubscribe, or until you ask us to delete it directly, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything inaccurate
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Withdraw consent to future emails at any time
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you believe your data has been mishandled, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint

To exercise any of these rights, contact contact@discernmentwealthacademy.co.uk.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change too. Significant changes will be reflected here as they happen.
